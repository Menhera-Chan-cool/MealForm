- Existing bounded numeric-only device layout stores a validated column permutation, keeping widths/hidden state by original column identity. All three tables retain semantic cells and correct rowspan/colspan grouping; mobile uses canonical full order. Grip/Left/Right/reset never modify data, filters or Sheet columns.
- GAS verifies stores independently after deletion/restoration, returning safe scoped receipts on success or business failure. Exact HMAC-bound identity, lock, permissions, duplicate guards, restore history and no-write-retry remain. Restore requires both returned response ID and contents; unknown submission outcomes stay unknown and cannot be retried blindly. Only same-time candidates have answers parsed, not every unrelated Form answer.
